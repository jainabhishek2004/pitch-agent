# Pitch structure & the mandatory "helps-specifically" formula

The `generate_pitch_pdf` tool renders 4 pages:
1. **Cover + client snapshot** — hook headline, industry, business model, classification chips.
2. **Challenges** — 3–6 pain points, each labelled `confirmed` or `likely` (default `likely` with no meeting notes).
3. **Recommended solution** — primary + secondary + expansion + AI layer, reasoning, and the helps-specifically box.
4. **Proof of fit & plan** — feature mapping (pain → feature → product → impact), AI agents, roadmap, CTA.

## Mandatory field — "How Hyperion helps this client specifically"
Always fill `howItHelpsSpecifically` using this formula:

> Because **[Client]** operates as a **[business model]**, Hyperion helps by **[connect the recommended products into one operating layer]**, so that they can **[concrete business outcomes]** — with live operational visibility.

**Example:** "Because ProcX operates as an industrial indirect-materials distributor, Hyperion helps by connecting Inventory and Warehouse into one operating layer, so that they can trust one stock number, see true landed cost on imports, and stop fulfilment running on WhatsApp — with live operational visibility."

## Quality bar
- Every product/feature/agent must come from `search_knowledge_base`.
- Pick only what fits. Primary + secondary + expansion + AI layer — never the whole catalog.
- Headlines are two parts; the second part is highlighted in the brand amber.
